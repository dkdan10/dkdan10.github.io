02. About

I'm a Columbia CS senior in the School of General Studies, graduating May 2027. I came back to school after two years as an Applications Engineer at Medidata Solutions, where I built patient-facing React components and Rails endpoints for the "Ship Direct to Patient" feature during the COVID lockdowns. I started in tech through App Academy in 2019.

Between Medidata and Columbia I spent two years as House Manager at a sober-living community in New York: daily operations, crisis response, accountability. I'm also a scout-team practice player for Columbia Women's Basketball. That work is where I learned to communicate under pressure and to care about what a tool actually does for the person using it.

The two flagship projects above were built with AI coding agents (Claude Code and Codex) as the implementers. My work was the specification, the architecture decisions, the evaluation methodology, and the acceptance gates — deciding what counts as done and refusing to ship what didn't clear the bar. Each project's README says the same.
